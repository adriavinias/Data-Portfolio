# Entregable 2: Supervised Machine Learning
## Business Context & Deliverables

MobileNOW, a telecommunications provider, is facing increased customer attrition. To optimize its telemarketing retention budget, the company requires an end-to-end Machine Learning solution to prioritize outreach efforts and identify long-term drivers of customer churn.

### Key Deliverables & Approach:

1. **Targeted Churn Propensity Scoring:**
   * Trained and tuned a binary classification model to output **churn probabilities** ($Churn = 1$).
   * Generated probability scores for a pre-selected cohort of **1,500 active customers** to rank and prioritize high-risk targets for the upcoming monthly retention campaign.

2. **Feature Importance & Strategic Drivers:**
   * Analyzed model weights and feature importances (e.g., via SHAP / Random Forest feature importance) to uncover the primary operational and pricing factors influencing customer cancellation.
   * Delivered actionable insights to assist long-term product and service improvement strategies.

> **Business Impact:** Enables transition from blanket marketing to a high-ROI, risk-prioritized retention campaign targeting the top candidates most likely to churn.
